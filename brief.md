## References used

### 1. Blind Barber (https://www.blindbarber.com)
- Hero composition: Large-format product/lifestyle imagery with minimal text overlay. Visual storytelling dominates over copy.
- Section order: Brand identity first, then product categories, then physical locations, then content hub, then social proof. Establishes credibility before driving action.
- Copy voice: Conversational yet aspirational. Moderate-length sentences. Emotional benefit language ("the best version of yourself"). Avoids corporate stiffness and casual slang equally.
- Photo treatment: Clean product shots with complementary lifestyle imagery. Professional styling with warm lighting. Male subjects appear well-groomed but relatable.
- Trust signals: Specific founder attribution ("Jeff Laub"), 13+ year heritage, press citations (Vanity Fair, NYT), 5+ city presence. Values pledge in header ("2% of purchases go to charity").
- Conversion path: Navigation flows discovery-first, commerce-second. Experiential positioning before product push.
- **Apply to this build:** (1) Hero that leads with atmosphere photo over text, (2) Founder attribution with specific name and years, (3) Warm, approachable but aspirational copy voice, (4) Trust signals use specific press/heritage dates not generic claims, (5) Values/community commitment surfaced early.

### 2. Byredo (https://www.byredo.com) [NON-COMPETITOR]
- Hero composition: Layered visual carousel with editorial, gallery-like aesthetic. Curated showcase approach rather than aggressive selling.
- Section order: Discovery and curation prioritized over direct conversion. Brand world before product catalog.
- Copy voice: Poetic, intimate. Campaign titles use metaphorical language. Aspirational lifestyle-brand cadence. Zero hard-sell messaging.
- Photo treatment: Minimalist still-life staging with soft focus, neutral backgrounds. Shallow depth-of-field. Warm, diffused lighting that conveys premium craftsmanship.
- Trust signals: Exclusive services (free shipping, returns, samples) positioned as brand confidence, not discount bait. Service integrity over urgency.
- Conversion path: Experiential offerings (gift wrapping, personalized messages, free samples) rather than discount-driven urgency.
- **Apply to this build:** (1) Editorial photo treatment with warm tones and intentional staging, (2) Restraint in copy - let the visuals carry weight, (3) Premium service positioning (hot towel shave as ritual, not just a service), (4) Minimal, deliberate typography with generous whitespace, (5) Gold accent used sparingly as luxury punctuation.

### Cross-reference design thesis
Lead with a full-atmosphere hero photo showing the barbershop environment (per Blind Barber's visual-first approach) on a dark, warm canvas (per Byredo's luxury staging). Copy stays restrained and aspirational - short sentences, specific credentials (4.9 stars, 80 reviews, founding details), no generic claims. Gold accent is used as punctuation, not decoration. Services are framed as rituals, not transactions. Single CTA per section routing to phone or booking.
